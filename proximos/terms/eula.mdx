## 4. Free Trial

Every new Proximos license includes a **60-day free trial**. No upfront commitment is required — your trial begins immediately upon purchase. Because Proximos can only be meaningfully evaluated against available macOS updates, the 60-day trial period is designed to allow sufficient time for Apple to release at least one or two macOS update cycles.

The following terms govern the free trial:

- Automatic tier upgrades from License Counts do **not** trigger during an active trial period.
- When your trial converts to a paid subscription, the tier is set to match your actual device count at the time of conversion.
- Only one free trial is permitted per email address.
- Cancellation of a trial at any time permanently forfeits that trial. Subsequent signup attempts using the same email address will be automatically converted to an active paid subscription immediately after subscribing

## 5. Educational Discount

Educational institutions are eligible for a **30% discount** on any Proximos subscription tier. The following terms govern the educational discount:

- Discount codes may be requested through the Proximos website. Submitting a request with an `.edu` email address will result in a discount code being emailed to your .edu address for use at checkout. Using a promotional code allows for a proper receipt to be displayed / emailed showing the discounted price. If you need to forward a receipt of purchase to an internal department, it is recommended to use a promotional code to show the discounted price.
- If you purchase Proximos using an `.edu` email address _without_ a discount code, the Proximos Licensing Engine will automatically apply the educational discount to your account within 24 hours.
- Any promotional or educational discount code applied to your subscription is honored on a continual basis - including through future automatic tier upgrades.
- The Proximos Engine performs daily audits of accounts with active educational discounts. If a customer account is found not to have an `.edu` address, but entered an educational promotional discount code at checkout, the educational discount will be automatically removed.

## 6. Automated Tier Upgrade Logic

The Proximos Engine tracks the number of unique Mac UUIDs that check in against each license key over rolling 30-day windows. The following automated upgrade logic applies:

- If your active Mac device count exceeds **110% of your current subscription tier's device limit** and sustains that level for **30 consecutive days**, your subscription will be automatically upgraded.
- The upgrade applies the tier that matches your actual active device count at your next billing cycle.
- Automated tier upgrades do not occur during an active free trial period (see Section 4).
